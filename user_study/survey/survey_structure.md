## Section 1: Participant Background
Usability evaluation focuses on how effectively users can interact with a system to achieve their goals. A well-designed interface improves user satisfaction, efficiency, and reduces errors. To assess usability, we often rely on established heuristics, such as *Nielsen’s Usability Heuristics*, which include:
* Visibility of system status
* Match between the system and the real world
* User control and freedom
* Consistency and standards
* Error prevention
* Recognition rather than recall
* Flexibility and efficiency of use
* Aesthetic and minimalist design
* Help users recognize, diagnose, and recover from errors
* Help and documentation

## Section 3: Review Usability Improvement 1
Please review the following usability improvement recommendation and answer the questions.

Follow the link if you want to replay the video: **anonymized**


Identified Usability Issue:

**The system allows account creation without verifying the email address first. This could lead to users creating accounts with typos in their email, preventing them from logging in later or receiving important notifications.**

Recommendation:

**Implement email verification during account creation. Send a verification link to the provided email address and require users to click the link before their account is fully activated.**
